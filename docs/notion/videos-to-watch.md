## A. CORE — mandatory for everybody in the lab

| Priority | Video | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Required** | **Oklahoma State University EHS – Lab Safety Training** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TlhVR0xQLJQ | University-specific general laboratory training from OSU EHS. |
| **Required** | **US Chemical Safety Board – Experimenting with Danger** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ALBWxGik64A | Academic-research safety video examining real university accidents (Texas Tech, Dartmouth, UCLA). |
| **Required** | **ACS – From Chemical Safety Rules to Risk Management (RAMP)** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DWSymRPCDN4 | Teaches **Recognize hazards → Assess risks → Minimize risks → Prepare for emergencies**, rather than memorizing rules. |

Make **Experimenting with Danger** mandatory even for mechanical and electrical students: it addresses novel experiments where no established industrial procedure exists.

## B. ELECTRICAL SAFETY — ESP training

### ESP-E1 — General electrical users

1. **Iowa State University EHS — Electrical Safety, Lab Safety Awareness Week 2026** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aMJ8XlBcshY
   Recent university EHS electrical-safety material.
2. **UNLV — Electrical & Computer Engineering Laboratory Safety** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28ixSnZ0ycE
   Relevant to students working with power supplies, circuits, test equipment and electrical benches.

### ESP-E2/E3 — Hazardous-energy equipment

3. **Oklahoma State University EHS — Control of Hazardous Energy / Lockout-Tagout** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HFJq3BGm8Kw
   For researchers who maintain equipment, modify rigs, service motors/pumps/heaters, or could encounter stored electrical/mechanical energy.

Require **E2/E3 authorization** for anyone working on exposed higher-voltage/high-current circuits, power electronics, battery packs or custom electrical cabinets.

## C. MECHANICAL / FABRICATION SAFETY

### MSP-M1 — General fabrication-shop entry

1. **University at Buffalo Engineering — Machine Shop Safety Orientation** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fb6oGz9gfIo
   UB uses it as required viewing (plus a test) before machine shop use.
2. **University of Utah Mechanical Engineering — Shop Safety** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=an-pRgxn1LM
   General intro; Utah notes **specific training is still required before machine-shop access**.

### MSP-M2 — Machine operators

3. **Oklahoma State University EHS — Machine Guarding** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7kj_jcUrObw
   Before operating rotating/cutting machinery or building automated rigs with moving parts.
4. **Oklahoma State University EHS — Lockout/Tagout** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HFJq3BGm8Kw
   Same LOTO video; equally important for CNC, lathe, mill, robots, motors, hydraulic/pneumatic systems, pumps, compressors.

Do **not** use YouTube as the actual lathe/mill/CNC certification. After the videos, the fabrication lead should physically demonstrate the machine and observe each student operating it.

## Certification in 5 steps

1. **Video training**
2. **Short quiz** — e.g. ≥80%
3. **Lab-specific orientation**
4. **Hands-on demonstration of competency**
5. **Equipment/process authorization recorded**

**"Training completed" and "authorized to operate" are two different things.** For example:

> **Lathe:** videos ✓ → quiz ✓ → shop orientation ✓ → supervised operation ✓ → **Lathe Authorized**

> **Hydrogen experiment:** chemical/gas training ✓ → gas-system SOP ✓ → leak/emergency demonstration ✓ → PI/EHS approval → **H₂ Authorized**
